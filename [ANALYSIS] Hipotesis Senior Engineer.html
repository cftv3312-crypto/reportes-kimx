EnunciadoStatement

El sistema de planeación opera con dos forecasts fundamentalmente incoherentes: ExF (Execution Forecast) está sistemáticamente subestimado 3.6× a 11× contra Producción real, y CSCH (Customer Schedule) = Producción exacta en el 100% de los casos, lo que indica que el schedule se ajusta al actual y no al revés. Resultado: no existe variance medible que permita aprender, mientras Procurement compra basado en señales infladas (FCH = 8.5× la producción real).

The planning system operates on two fundamentally inconsistent forecasts: ExF (Execution Forecast) is systematically undercast 3.6× to 11× vs real Production, and CSCH (Customer Schedule) = exact Production in 100% of cases, which indicates schedule adjusts to actual rather than the reverse. Result: no measurable variance that enables learning, while Procurement buys against inflated signals (FCH = 8.5× real production).

The aggregate reported utilization of 7.7% is misleading for three combined reasons: (a) 5CA Mega has Line Rate = 0 in master data — its 149 SKUs (33% of production) are excluded from the calculation; (b) FCH = 139M units represents nominal theoretical capacity, not operable; (c) per-SKU utilization dilutes because only 211 of 475 SKUs produced. Real shop-floor utilization per machine/shift is likely in typical molding ranges (50-75%), but no measurement today demonstrates it.
