✓ Positive Signal: SBO% Improving

SBO (molding quality) improved from 93.0% (2025) to 94.5% (2026) for the same weeks — suggesting the molding side is actually improving in-process control. The PPMS problem may originate upstream (material, tooling) rather than operator error.

⚠ Worst 2026 Week: Wk11 at 178.1 PPMS

Week 11 (2026) is the single worst outlier — 178 PPMS vs 85.5 in 2025 for the same week. A 2× spike on stable volume is a process excursion flag. Investigate mold condition, resin batch, and shift composition for that specific week.
